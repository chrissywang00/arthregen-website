## 0. About this document

This is the first comprehensive sketch of the ArthRegen brand system. It is a working draft, not a final guideline. Strategic decisions are locked; visual specifications and voice samples are directional and meant to be iterated.

The document is organized to be read top-to-bottom on the first pass. Strategy and verbal identity come before visual identity because every visual decision should be derivable from the strategic foundation. If a visual choice cannot be traced back to one of the four brand pillars, it is decoration, not identity.

A note on color values: hex codes in Section 5 are sampled approximations from the existing logo PNG. Before any production application, sample the values directly from the source vector file (.ai, .svg, or .pdf) to lock the official palette.

## 1. Strategic foundation

### 1.1 Who ArthRegen is

ArthRegen is a regenerative medicine platform spun out of Samsung Medical Center, founded by an orthopedic surgeon. The company designs personalized cartilage and meniscus regeneration solutions that combine stem cell therapeutics, AI-driven anatomical modeling, and 3D printing. Its long-term vision is to replace metal joint replacement with bio-native, regenerative alternatives.

ArthRegen is not a typical biotech startup. It was founded inside a major hospital, by a surgeon who has performed over 13,000 procedures, with a clinical advisory board that reads like a directory of Korea's top orthopedic departments. This provenance is the brand's most defensible differentiator and should be reinforced at every consequential touchpoint.

### 1.2 The four pillars

Every brand decision flows from these four pillars. They are the lens through which copy, design, and product communication are evaluated.

**Surgeon-designed.** ArthRegen was built from the operating room outward, not from the lab inward. The science is rigorous because the surgical reality demanded it. This pillar carries clinical authority and credibility.

**Precision-engineered.** AI-driven patient-specific design, controlled 3D printing, manufacturing rigor. Personalization is the default, not a feature. This pillar carries technical excellence.

**Bio-native.** The materials are the body's own. Allogeneic cells, decellularized human tissue, biodegradable scaffolds engineered to integrate. Low immunogenicity is both a science fact and a brand promise. This pillar carries safety and trust.

**Movement restored.** The outcome. The reason any of this matters. A knee that bends. A morning walk regained. A grandchild lifted. This pillar carries human meaning and is the home of "Healing motion, Restoring moments."

The first three pillars are how ArthRegen earns the right to make the fourth claim.

### 1.3 Mission, vision, promise

**Mission.** To restore natural joint function through personalized regenerative medicine.

**Vision.** A future in which damaged joints are regenerated, not replaced.

**Promise.** Every product ArthRegen ships is designed by surgeons, engineered for the individual patient, and built from biologically native materials.

## 2. Verbal identity

### 2.1 Brand name

The company is **ArthRegen** in English and **아스리젠** in Korean. The legal entity is **(주)아스리젠 / ArthRegen Co., Ltd.**

In running text, write *ArthRegen* with a capital A and capital R, no space, no hyphen. Never *Arthregen*, *Arth-Regen*, *ARTHREGEN*, or *Arth Regen*. The capitalization makes the etymology legible: *Arth-* (joint, from Greek *arthron*) plus *-Regen* (regeneration).

In Korean copy, *아스리젠* is written without the parenthetical *(주)* unless the legal entity must be specified.

### 2.2 Tagline

**English (canonical):** Healing motion, Restoring moments.

**Korean (canonical):** 움직임을 치유하고, 순간을 되찾다.

The two are sibling lines, not literal translations. Both work as standalone signatures and as scene-closing lines in longer copy. Always set with the comma and the parallel structure intact. Do not break across more than two lines unless layout demands it.

The tagline lives at the bottom of the hierarchy in most applications. It is not a headline. It is a signature. Use it sparingly so it retains weight.

### 2.3 Product naming

ArthRegen has three products in the current pipeline. Each name follows a different convention by accident of development history; that is acceptable and should be preserved.

**Carti-All** — the cell therapy platform. Hyphenated. *Carti* references cartilage; *All* signals the inclusivity of the regenerative approach. In Korean materials, write as *Carti-All* (Latin) with descriptor *관절연골 세포치료제* nearby on first mention.

**MeniSave** — the patient-specific meniscus implant. One word, two capitals. *Meni* references meniscus; *Save* signals preservation of native tissue and function.

**MeniFul** — the cartilage and meniscus regeneration therapeutic. One word, two capitals. *Meni* + *-Ful* signals the fullness or completeness of regeneration.

When listing the product portfolio, default order: **Carti-All, MeniSave, MeniFul** (alphabetical). When ordering by go-to-market sequence: **MeniFul, MeniSave, Carti-All** (matching the 2027/2029/2030 launch cadence).

### 2.4 Platform versus products

Lead with the platform, support with the products. The brand is ArthRegen; the products are the proof. Investor and clinician communications should foreground the integrated regenerative platform thesis (treatment, replacement, regeneration unified on one AI-driven system) rather than treating the three products as a disconnected pipeline.

A useful internal phrasing: *"ArthRegen is the platform. Carti-All, MeniSave, and MeniFul are the first three expressions of it."*

## 3. Voice and tone

### 3.1 Core voice attributes

ArthRegen's voice is **clinically precise**, **quietly confident**, and **humanely grounded**. It earns trust through specificity, not adjectives. It does not oversell. It does not soften its science to seem accessible. It writes the way a senior surgeon explains their work to a smart non-specialist: respectful of the reader's intelligence, generous with structure, sparing with superlatives.

### 3.2 Three audience modes

The voice has one personality and three calibrations. The personality does not change between modes; the register does.

**Investor mode.** Ambitious, declarative, data-led. This is the voice of the IR deck and the investor section of the website. Numbers carry the message. Claims are framed against the competitive set. Korean register: 정중하고 분석적, 명확한 수치 중심. This is the closest mode to the existing IR deck's voice.

**Clinician mode.** Precise, evidence-led, peer-to-peer. This is the voice of the science pages, technical documentation, and KOL communication. Mechanism comes before benefit. Citations are visible. No marketing copy in this mode. Korean register: 의학적으로 정확하고 절제된, 동료 의사에게 말하는 톤.

**Patient mode.** Warm, plain-language, human. This is the voice of patient-facing pages, eventually patient education materials. Medical vocabulary is translated, never assumed. The tagline lives natively in this mode. Korean register: 따뜻하지만 가볍지 않고, 환자의 경험을 존중하는 톤. Avoid the over-flowery 의료광고 register.

### 3.3 Voice in practice

**Investor mode example (EN).** "ArthRegen integrates three regenerative platforms — cell therapy, patient-specific implants, and decellularized tissue — under a single AI-driven design system. Carti-All reduces manufacturing time by 40% versus the current 4th-generation standard while increasing collagen II content."

**Clinician mode example (EN).** "MeniSave is a bioresorbable, patient-specific meniscus scaffold engineered through layered offset 3D printing. The structure replicates the hoop tension distribution of native meniscus, with mechanical properties verified across animal study cohorts."

**Patient mode example (EN).** "The meniscus is the part of your knee that absorbs impact when you walk. When it tears, doctors usually have to remove it. We are developing a way to replace it instead, using a scaffold built from your own anatomy."

**환자 모드 예시 (KR).** "반월연골판은 무릎에서 충격을 흡수하는 조직입니다. 기존 치료에서는 손상된 반월연골판을 절제하는 경우가 많았지만, 아스리젠은 환자 개개인의 무릎 구조에 맞춘 이식체로 이를 대체하는 기술을 개발하고 있습니다."

### 3.4 Words we use

*Regenerate. Restore. Engineer. Design. Personalize. Native. Integrate. Precision. Evidence.*

### 3.5 Words we avoid

*Revolutionary. Cutting-edge. World-class. Game-changer. Disrupt. Breakthrough* (unless attached to specific regulatory designation, e.g., FDA Breakthrough Designation). *Innovative* unless paired with what specifically is innovative.

In Korean: avoid 혁신적, 획기적, 세계 최고 used as floating modifiers. The IR deck uses some of these for momentum; in brand-owned channels they should be replaced with specifics.

### 3.6 Bilingual considerations

Write each language natively. Do not translate one into the other and call it done. A line that works in English ("Healing motion, Restoring moments") and its Korean sibling ("움직임을 치유하고, 순간을 되찾다") should each be evaluated against their own language's native rhythm.

When typesetting bilingual content side by side, give Korean text slightly more line height (1.6 versus 1.5 for Latin) to accommodate the visual weight of Hangul. Korean and Latin should share a baseline grid wherever practical.

## 4. Logo system

### 4.1 Existing assets

ArthRegen has three logo assets currently in use, all shown in the brand banner and IR deck.

The **mark** is the geometric A composed of two triangular forms with a circular element in the upper-right negative space, set in a blue-to-teal gradient.

The **horizontal lockup** pairs the mark with the wordmark *ArthRegen* set in a humanist sans-serif in deep navy.

The **stacked lockup** is implied by the IR deck's cover treatment but should be formalized as an asset.

### 4.2 Logo variants to formalize

The system needs the following variants produced as locked artwork:

- Full color horizontal (mark + wordmark)
- Full color stacked (mark above wordmark)
- Mark only, full color
- Single color navy (for limited-color print, embossing, etching)
- Single color white (for use on dark backgrounds)
- Single color black (for fax, photocopy, regulatory documents)

### 4.3 Clear space

The minimum clear space around any logo lockup is equal to the height of the circle in the mark. This applies to all four sides. No element — image, text, edge of frame — should enter this zone.

### 4.4 Minimum size

Horizontal lockup: 120px digital, 30mm print.
Mark only: 32px digital, 8mm print.

Below these thresholds, the gradient detail and the circle's relationship to the triangle become illegible.

### 4.5 Logo don'ts

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or recompose the lockup. Do not change the gradient direction or substitute colors. Do not place the full-color logo on any background that compromises gradient legibility (busy photography, mid-blue fields, gradients of any kind). When in doubt, use the single-color white version on dark imagery.

## 5. Color

### 5.1 The palette is strict

ArthRegen uses a disciplined blue-teal palette with neutrals. There is no accent color. Data visualization, callouts, and emphasis all live within the existing range. This discipline is a deliberate brand decision: most biotech brands look the same because they default to the same accent palette. Restraint is differentiation.

### 5.2 Brand colors

Sampled approximations from the existing logo. Confirm against source vector before production.

| Name | Hex (approx.) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| ArthRegen Navy | #1E3A8A | Primary type, wordmark color, primary UI elements |
| ArthRegen Blue | #2168A0 | Mid-tone, gradient anchor, secondary type |
| ArthRegen Teal | #2DB1A4 | Accent within range, gradient endpoint, data highlights |

### 5.3 Neutrals

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #0F172A | Body type on light backgrounds |
| Slate | #475569 | Secondary type, captions |
| Mist | #E2E8F0 | Dividers, subtle backgrounds |
| Bone | #F8FAFC | Page backgrounds, surface fills |
| White | #FFFFFF | Primary surface |

### 5.4 Usage hierarchy

The dominant surface in any composition should be white or Bone. Navy carries type weight. Blue and Teal appear as accents, gradient passages, and data visualization. The gradient itself is a brand asset (see 5.5) and should be reserved for moments that earn it.

A useful proportional rule: 70% neutral / 20% navy / 10% blue-teal-and-gradient. Compositions that invert this ratio start to feel like SaaS marketing, not medical infrastructure.

### 5.5 Gradient rules

The signature ArthRegen gradient runs from ArthRegen Navy through ArthRegen Blue to ArthRegen Teal. It can be applied at any angle but should always read as a continuous flow, never banded.

The gradient is a connective element, not a default surface. Use it for hero backgrounds, section transitions, the mark itself, and occasional large display moments. Do not use it as a button fill, a card background, or anywhere it competes with content.

The flowing organic gradient already established in the brand banner (the watercolor-like extension behind "Healing motion, Restoring moments") is canonical. It should be developed as a small library of three to five reusable gradient compositions, all derived from the same color logic but varied in form, so the brand has gradient assets that don't repeat literally.

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 Bilingual typography architecture

ArthRegen operates in Korean and English. The locked type system pairs **Inter** (Latin) with **Pretendard** (Korean), with **IBM Plex Mono** for technical and data accents. The system was selected after evaluating three directions: a premium neo-grotesque option (Söhne or IBM Plex Sans), a humanist workhorse (Inter), and a dual-family structure-and-flow option (Manrope display + Source Sans body). The humanist workhorse direction was selected for three reasons: it pairs cleanly with the existing wordmark, it has zero licensing cost across web and print, and Inter and Pretendard share x-height logic that makes mixed-language settings feel like one system rather than two.

### 6.2 Latin: Inter

**Inter** carries display, body, and UI type across the brand. It is open source, screen-optimized, and available across web, desktop, and design tools. It pairs cleanly with the existing wordmark without competing with it.

Display weight: 600 (SemiBold) for headlines and large display moments.
Body weight: 400 (Regular) for running text, 500 (Medium) for emphasis and UI labels.
Caption weight: 400 (Regular).

### 6.3 Korean: Pretendard

**Pretendard** is the locked Korean typeface. It is open source, engineered specifically to harmonize with Latin sans-serifs at shared x-heights, and is the de facto standard for bilingual Korean-English digital design. Inter and Pretendard were chosen as a pair, not as separate decisions.

Display weight: 600 (SemiBold) for headlines.
Body weight: 400 (Regular) for body, 500 (Medium) for emphasis.

### 6.4 Mono accent: IBM Plex Mono

**IBM Plex Mono** carries technical accents: data callouts, regulatory references, technical specifications, code-like elements, captions on scientific content, and any moment where the brand needs to signal precision and machine-readable rigor. It is open source and visually compatible with Inter at body sizes.

Body weight: 400 (Regular).

Use IBM Plex Mono sparingly. It is an accent face, not a body face. When in doubt, set type in Inter.

### 6.5 The wordmark question (open)

The existing *ArthRegen* wordmark is set in a humanist sans (likely Mulish or close), not in Inter. Two paths forward:

**Option A: Keep the existing wordmark as a fixed brand asset.** The wordmark earns its distinctiveness by being slightly different from the body type. Inter handles everything around it. This is the conservative choice and the recommended default.

**Option B: Rebuild the wordmark in Inter SemiBold.** Total system consistency. The brand looks like one continuous voice across logo and copy. Slightly less distinctive, but cleaner.

This needs to be resolved before the website design phase. The decision affects logo files, application templates, and any future custom mark work.

### 6.6 Hierarchy (web reference)

| Level | Latin | Korean | Size (desktop) | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Inter SemiBold | Pretendard SemiBold | 56px | 1.05 |
| H1 | Inter SemiBold | Pretendard SemiBold | 40px | 1.15 |
| H2 | Inter SemiBold | Pretendard SemiBold | 28px | 1.25 |
| H3 | Inter Medium | Pretendard Medium | 20px | 1.3 |
| Body | Inter Regular | Pretendard Regular | 16px | 1.6 / KR 1.7 |
| Small | Inter Regular | Pretendard Regular | 14px | 1.5 / KR 1.6 |
| Mono / data | IBM Plex Mono Regular | — | 13px | 1.5 |
| Caption | Inter Regular | Pretendard Regular | 13px | 1.4 |

Tracking: tighten by -0.015em to -0.018em on display sizes (40px and above); 0 (default) at body sizes.

### 6.7 Setting rules

Body type is set in Ink (#0F172A) on light surfaces, white on Navy. Avoid setting body type in ArthRegen Blue or Teal — the contrast is insufficient for sustained reading.

Headlines may be set in Navy or in white on dark or gradient surfaces. Reserve Teal for occasional accent words within longer headlines, never an entire heading.

Korean text receives 1.7 line height as default; Latin receives 1.6. When mixing scripts in a single block, set the entire block to 1.7.

IBM Plex Mono accents are set in Slate (#475569) at small sizes by default, Ink at larger sizes. Avoid setting Mono in brand colors except in specific data visualization contexts.

## 7. Imagery

### 7.1 Three lanes of imagery

ArthRegen's image system has three distinct lanes, each doing different work. They are not interchangeable.

**Anatomical-scientific.** Tissue micrographs, scaffold renders, anatomical diagrams, MRI sections, the actual product visuals from research. This is the lane that carries proof. It is visually dense, often unbeautiful in a conventional sense, and that is correct.

**Human-in-motion.** Real people in the moments the tagline references: walking, hiking, kneeling in a garden, lifting a child, climbing stairs without thought. Natural light, observational framing, Korean and global representation. This is the lane that carries promise.

**Procedural-clinical.** Operating rooms, surgeons working, hands handling instruments. Restrained and respectful. Never staged-heroic. This is the lane that carries credibility, particularly in clinician communication.

### 7.2 Photography do's

Use natural light wherever possible. Frame humans observationally, not theatrically. Hands, knees, motion fragments often communicate more than full-body portraits. Diversity of age and ethnicity is essential — degenerative arthritis is global, and the patient base is not Korean alone.

### 7.3 Photography don'ts

No glowing DNA helices. No cells suspended in blue liquid. No doctors in lobby corridors holding tablets. No families gathered in soft-focus living rooms. No pharmaceutical-stock smiling-elderly-couple-walking-on-beach. These images are visual noise that signals "biotech brand" without communicating anything specific to ArthRegen.

### 7.4 Illustration and rendering

Technical illustrations should follow a flat, geometric, accurate style — closer to a medical journal figure than to a marketing rendering. Use the brand palette. When 3D renders of product (scaffolds, implants) are necessary, render against neutral backgrounds with controlled lighting; let the geometry of the object carry the image.

AI-generated imagery is not currently approved for any brand-owned channel. This is a conservative stance for a medical company and should be revisited only with clear rationale.

## 8. Iconography and graphic language

### 8.1 Icon style

Linear, single-weight, geometric icons drawn at 24px and 32px reference sizes. Stroke weight 1.5px at 24px. Rounded line caps and joins. Single color (Navy or Teal depending on context). No filled icons in the primary system.

This style sits adjacent to the wordmark and mark without competing with them.

### 8.2 The mark as system element

The triangular peak motif from the logo can be extracted and used as a system element: section dividers, page numerals, list markers, callout indicators. Use it sparingly. Once a viewer registers the connection between the system element and the mark, the connection is made; further repetition diminishes it.

The IR deck already uses this approach in its chapter divider pages (the watermarked triangle in the lower right). That treatment should be codified and extended.

### 8.3 Diagram style for technical content

Technical diagrams (mechanism of action, manufacturing process, regulatory pathway) should follow a consistent visual logic: process flows left-to-right, comparison tables organized by attribute rows, data visualizations using the strict palette with ArthRegen Teal as the highlight color for ArthRegen's own data points against Slate-grey competitor or reference data.

Reference: the IR deck's competitive comparison tables (Carti-All vs. Cartistem, MeniSave vs. NUsurface) are already close to the right model. They should be retemplated in the locked color and type system.

## 9. Motion principles

Motion is not a primary deliverable in this draft, but the principles are worth establishing now so that any motion produced (web, social, video) reads as ArthRegen.

**Calm.** Movement is unhurried. No bouncy easing curves, no aggressive choreography. Cubic-bezier curves favor long approaches and gentle settles.

**Restorative.** Where the gradient moves, it flows like water or breath. Continuous, looping, never spiking.

**Purposeful.** Motion serves comprehension. If a transition does not help the viewer understand a relationship between two states, remove it.

**Restrained in the clinical lane.** Procedural and scientific content should be near-static. Motion belongs in the patient and investor lanes; the clinician lane stays composed.
